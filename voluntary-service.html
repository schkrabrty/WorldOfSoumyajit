Academic Service

Below, I list my academic service experiences over the years.

I have served as a reviewer for leading venues, including the IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, the International Journal of Human-Computer Interaction, the Computers in Human Behavior Reports, and the ACM CHI 2025, IEEE ISMAR 2025, 2026, IEEE VR 2026, and IEEE Telepresence 2026 conferences. I strive to provide thoughtful and constructive feedback that helps advance the quality and rigor of research in our field. I have also served as an international program committee member of the IEEE VR 2026 and IEEE ISMAR 2026 conferences.
